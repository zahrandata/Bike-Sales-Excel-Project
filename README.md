# Bike Sales Data Analysis & Interactive Dashboard Excel Project

## Workbook overview & structure

This workbook contains 4 sheets about the bike sales dataset:

* **Bike_Buyers_Original_Dataset**
  Raw, unedited dataset.

* **Working_Sheet_Clean_Dataset**
  Cleaned dataset with removed duplicates, standardized labels, and an added Age Brackets column.

* **Pivot_Table**
  Summary tables and charts analyzing income, commute distance, age, and occupation vs. bike purchases.

* **Dashboard**
  Interactive dashboard with visualizations and slicers (Marital Status, Region, Education, Homeowner) for dynamic filtering.

## Key Insights

* **Income vs. Purchasing Power:** Buyers across both genders have higher average incomes ($60,124 for males and $55,774 for females) compared to non-buyers ($56,208 for males and $53,440 for females).
* **Commute Sensitivity:** Customers with short commutes (0–1 miles) represent the highest volume of bike buyers (200 buyers), while purchase intent drops significantly for long-distance commutes (10+ miles).
* **Core Demographics:** Middle-aged individuals (31–54) dominate bike purchases with 383 buyers, far outperforming Adolescent (39) and Older (59) customer segments.
* **Occupational Groups:** Professional (150 buyers) and Skilled Manual (115 buyers) occupations account for the largest purchase volume.
* **Interactive Slicers:** The dashboard includes dynamic filtering for Marital Status, Region, Education, and Homeowner status to explore localized demographic subsets.
